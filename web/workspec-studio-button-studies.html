Rail button states

Seven treatments for the workspace buttons — hover and selected. Every rail below is live: move across it to feel the hover state, click to set the selection. Judge each one in context, against the canvas surface.

01 · Tint

Cyan Tint

Invites the brand accent into the state itself instead of a single side bar. Hover is a faint cyan wash; selection deepens the wash and inverts the icon chip to solid cyan. The calmest way to make the accent do real work.

HOVERwash 9% · border 30%
ACTIVEwash 16% · chip solid cyan

accent

02 · Tactile

Machined Key

Leans into the tool-like chrome: buttons behave like physical keys. Hover raises the key off the rail; selection presses it in — inset shadow, depressed surface, lit cyan chip. Selection reads through touch logic, not color alone.

HOVERlift -1px · drop shadow
ACTIVEpressed inset · chip solid cyan

03 · Motion

Edge Slide

The current cyan bar, but given choreography. Hover previews the bar as a translucent ghost that stretches in; selection snaps it to full strength with a spring. Selection surface goes white so the bar is the only thing carrying state.

HOVERghost bar 38% · scaleY .35→1
ACTIVEsolid bar · spring 220ms · bg white

04 · Elevation

White Card

Selection as a raised surface: the active button floats off the gray rail as a white card with a hairline cyan keyline, like a segmented control. Hover is the same card at half opacity. Highest-contrast selection with zero fill.

HOVERghost card 55%
ACTIVEwhite card · shadow · keyline 28%

05 · Structural

Underline Tab

Treats rail buttons as tabs into the workspace. Selection turns the button white and drops a 2px cyan underline that grows from the left, squaring off the bottom corners so the button appears connected to the content surface. Hover previews with a neutral hairline.

HOVERhairline preview · scaleX 0→1
ACTIVEcyan underline · bg white · opens downward

06 · Contrast

Ink Signal

The boldest option: selection inverts. The active button switches to dark ink with light copy, and the chip ignites cyan with a soft glow — unmistakable at a glance, and it echoes the dark accents already in dialogs. Hover stays quiet so the inversion lands harder.

HOVER#f2f0ee · chip ring 22%
ACTIVEink #2b2830 · chip glow cyan

All studies use the studio's real geometry (220px rail · 52px buttons · 32px chips) and palette (#e9e7e5 chrome · #16bdb9 cyan · #211f25 ink · Source Sans 3 / IBM Plex Mono). Any of them can be applied to .uaw-rail-button in playground-v2.css — hover and .active selectors only, no markup changes. The frozen-states toggle restages every rail with a rest, hovered, and selected button side by side for direct comparison.
